2026 Minnesota Climate Action Framework Dashboard

First created in 2022 and updated in 2026, the Minnesota Climate Action Framework (CAF) is the state’s plan to reduce carbon pollution and prepare Minnesota’s communities for climate change in ways that benefit all Minnesotans. The plan outlines 374 action steps across 7 different goals to achieve climate resilience in Minnesota.

The University of Minnesota Climate Adaptation Partnership (MCAP) created the CAF Dashboard to help Minnesotans explore the CAF goals and action steps. Use this dashboard to:

  • Explore all 7 goals and their respective initiatives, sub-initiatives, and action steps in the goal tabs.
  • Search for terms across all 7 goals in the search bar.
  • Filter action steps by adaptation or mitigation categories in the filter bar.

This is not an official state resource. Adaptation and mitigation filters were categorized by MCAP and are not part of the original CAF. Always reference the original state documents.
